## 3. Recurring difficulties

Ordered roughly by how often they come up:

1. **Word order in the middle field — now the main problem.** Three errors on 2026-08-27 while every case ending was correct.
   - **Time → Manner → Place (TeMoLo)**: *in München seit letztem Jahr*, *zum Arzt mit seinem Bruder*. Corrected and then applied right four exercises later.
   - **Pronoun objects move left**, ahead of adverbs and time expressions: *morgen mich*, *gern dich*.
2. **Vocabulary retrieval failures repeating across sessions** — *sich verabschieden*, *sich beeilen*, *verlassen*, *wiedersehen*, *das Fenster*. Supplied rather than produced, several for the second session running.
3. **Preposition choice** (as opposed to case, which is solid):
   - **aus** vs. **von** — *vom Hause* for "out of the house"
   - **nach** vs. **in** for movement to a city — wrong in two consecutive sessions
4. **das Haus with a stray -e** — *das Hause*, *von Hause*, *vom Hause* across three consecutive sessions. The *-e* survives only in *zu Hause* and *nach Hause*.
5. **Preposition vs. conjunction** — *nach* + Dativ vs. *nachdem* + full clause; *vor* vs. *bevor*. Wrote *Bevor ins Bett gehen*. Related: fronted **phrase** takes no comma, fronted **clause** does.
6. **Verb case governance — the dative leak.** *fragen* and *bitten* take Akkusativ; *mir* was written for both. Source is probably *versprechen*. Note *helfen* + Dativ was then correct, so the distinction is forming.
7. **Subject–verb agreement slips under load** — *wir muss*, *wir hat*, *sie gehst*. Improving: two correct under load later the same day.
8. **Reflexive pronoun dropped under load** — improving; two clean productions after correction.
9. **Adjective and article endings in case agreement** — though a dative adjective ending (*seit letztem Jahr*) was produced unaided on 2026-08-27.
10. **sein vs. haben in the Perfekt** for change-of-state verbs.
11. **Konjunktiv II der Vergangenheit** — infinitive used instead of past participle.

## 8. Format that works

- **One exercise at a time**, not batches presented all at once — explicitly requested.
- Sets of **20 items**, worked through sequentially.
- **Corrections requested explicitly**, including on spoken/written attempts.
- When practising in German, **slower pace** was requested.
- **English prompts for zu / um…zu exercises must use plain "to", never "in order to"** — otherwise the English cues the construction.
- Commit progress mid-session on request, not only at the end. GitHub writes require Gonçalo's approval each time; reads do not.
- Seeding exercises with low-scoring vocabulary from `vocabulary.md` works well — it surfaced *die Besprechung*, *helfen*, *liegen*, and *sich treffen* as new material without a separate vocabulary drill.
