## 3. Recurring difficulties

1. **Subject–verb agreement slips under load.** Four on 2026-08-27: *wir muss*, *wir hat*, *Her* for *Er*, *sprechen* for *spricht*. Plus *sie gehst* in session 7. **ZERO agreement errors in session 9** — first clean session on this. Watch whether it holds.
2. **Word order in the middle field.** TeMoLo wrong in ex. 22, 23, 30 — but **correct unprompted three times in session 9** (ex. 48, 55, 57). Improving.
3. **Weak nouns (n-declension) — NEW, session 9.** *Kollege* → *Kollegen* missed twice (ex. 52, 55). The noun's own case ending is redundant next to the article, so it feels droppable. Full class explained on request.
4. **Gender feeding the endings** — *meiner Bruder* → *meinem* (ex. 42), *zur Arzt* → *zum* (ex. 29, now repaired). The ending itself is right; the gender behind it breaks.
5. **Plusquamperfekt still not produced with *bevor*/*nachdem*** — ex. 59 wrote *hat gekauft* for "had bought". Recurring across sessions.
6. **Fixed prepositions** — *Angst von* → *Angst vor*. Welded to the verb/noun.
7. **Bare-dative verbs** — wrote *für ein neuen Kollege* for *schreiben* (ex. 52), then produced the bare dative unaided with *kaufen* two items later (ex. 54). Half-internalized.
8. **Preposition vs. conjunction** — *nach*/*nachdem*, *vor*/*bevor*. Still undrilled.
9. **English bleeding in under load** — *Her*, *We*, *Munich*, and *I* for *Ich* (ex. 56).
10. **Obligatory contractions** — *in dem* → *im* missed (ex. 53), though *zum* and *am* are solid.

## 8. Format that works

- **One exercise at a time**, not batches — explicitly requested.
- Sets of **20 items**, worked through sequentially.
- **Corrections requested explicitly.**
- **English prompts for zu / um…zu must use plain "to", never "in order to"** — otherwise the English cues the construction.
- **Transliteration: ASCII substitutes are CORRECT and must never be flagged** — *ss* = ß, *ae* = ä, *oe* = ö, *ue* = ü. Confirmed explicitly 2026-08-28.
- Commit progress mid-session on request. GitHub writes require Gonçalo's approval each time; reads do not.
- Seeding exercises with low-scoring vocabulary from `vocabulary.md` works well.
- **Explaining the underlying system on request lands well.** Strong/weak verbs, the ein-word ending table, and the full weak-noun class were all asked for mid-session and all produced immediate correct output.
- **Repeating a previously-failed sentence verbatim works.** Ex. 43 reused the ex. 40 sentence and got it right; the diagnosis was confirmed rather than assumed.
