# ARTICLE AND ADJECTIVE ENDINGS — opened 2026-08-27, DRILLED 2026-08-28 (session 9)

## ein-words (ein, kein, mein, dein, sein, ihr)

| | masc | neut | fem | plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nom** | ein | ein | eine | keine |
| **Akk** | ein**en** | ein | eine | keine |
| **Dat** | ein**em** | ein**em** | ein**er** | kein**en** |

Only the masculine changes between Nominativ and Akkusativ.

## Adjective endings after an ein-word

The adjective picks up whatever the article failed to carry. **-en is the default**; only five slots deviate:
- masc nom → *ein klein**er** Tisch*
- neut nom/akk → *ein klein**es** Haus*
- fem nom/akk → *eine klein**e** Wohnung*
- everything else → **-en**

## STATUS AFTER SESSION 9 — topic essentially repaired

- **Feminine accusative: 6/7.** One slip at ex. 59 (*eine neuen Jacke*), under the heaviest load of the set — load-dependent, not a gap.
- **Masculine accusative *einen -en*: 5/5** (ex. 44, 46, 50, 56, 60).
- **Neuter accusative bare + adjective *-es*: 3/3** (ex. 42, 45, 54).
- **Dative errors, the remaining weak area:**
  - Ex. 42: *meiner Bruder* → **meinem Bruder**. Masculine dative takes *-em*; *-er* is the feminine dative slot.
  - Ex. 46: *einer junger Frau* → **einer jungen Frau**. The article already carries the case, so the adjective falls back to the default *-en*. *-er* on the ADJECTIVE only ever appears in masculine nominative.
  - Ex. 52: *ein neuen Kollege* → **einem neuen Kollegen**.
- **Correct dative slots:** *ihrem kleinen Bruder* (ex. 48), *einem kleinen Balkon* (ex. 53), *seiner Mutter* (ex. 54), *einem alten Kollegen* (ex. 55), *in einer kleinen Wohnung* (ex. 57).

**Pattern: accusative is solved. Dative endings fail when the GENDER is misjudged, not when the table is misremembered.**

## Comparative + case ending stacked — ERROR ex. 49, STILL NOT DRILLED

*eine grösser Wohnung* → **eine größere Wohnung**. Two endings stack: comparative **-er** on the stem (*groß → größer*, umlaut), then the case ending on top.
- *eine große Wohnung* → *eine größere Wohnung*
- *einen großen Balkon* → *einen größeren Balkon*

The trap: comparative *-er* looks like a finished adjective ending. It is not.

# PREPOSITIONS

## Always Dativ, no decision needed

**aus, außer, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu** — never take any other case.

**bei** — the employer/premises preposition: *bei der Firma*, *beim Arzt* (at the doctor's) vs. *zum Arzt* (going there), *bei mir*.

## Obligatory contractions — ERROR ex. 53

*von + dem* → **vom** · *zu + dem* → **zum** · *zu + der* → **zur** · *an + dem* → **am** · *in + dem* → **im** · *bei + dem* → **beim** · *an + das* → **ans** · *in + das* → **ins**

- **zur** = *zu + der* → FEMININE only
- **zum** = *zu + dem* → masculine and neuter

Ex. 29: *zur Arzt* → **zum Arzt**. **REPAIRED ex. 48.**
Ex. 53: *in dem Wohnzimmer* → **im Wohnzimmer**. The contraction is obligatory, not optional. *zum* and *am* are solid, so the mechanism is known — it just does not fire on *in*.

## aus vs. von — RESOLVED 2026-08-27

- **aus** = emerging from *inside* an enclosed space: *aus dem Haus, aus der Tasche*
- **von** = away from a point or surface: *vom Bahnhof, vom Tisch*

## Two-way prepositions — A STRENGTH

Dativ for location (*wo?*), Akkusativ for movement (*wohin?*). **The verb decides.**

- **Ex. 36: *an die Wand gehängt* (Akk.) and *über dem Sofa* (Dat.) in ONE sentence, both correct unaided.**
- Ex. 58: *vor dem Haus* — static, Dativ. Correct unaided.

## Dative plural adds -n to the NOUN — ERROR ex. 57

Whenever the plural doesn't already end in *-n* or *-s*: *mit den Freunden, mit den Kindern, aus den Häusern, zwischen den Fenstern*.
Correct ex. 32, ex. 37. **Dropped at ex. 57: *mit zwei Kinder* → mit zwei Kindern.** The numeral replaced the article and removed the visual cue — the *-n* still applies.

## Countries and cities

**nach** for countries without an article and all cities: *nach Italien* (correct ex. 56), *nach München*. Exceptions take *in + Akk.*: *in die Schweiz, in die Türkei, in die USA*.

## Time prepositions

- **um** — clock times · **am** — days/parts of day · **im** — months/seasons · **seit** — + Dativ · bare — *nächste Woche, jeden Tag*

# VERBS

## Strong / weak / mixed

- **Weak** = regular. *legen – legte – hat gelegt*. **-te** past, **ge- + stem + -t** participle.
- **Strong** = irregular. Stem vowel shifts: *liegen – lag – hat gelegen*. Participle in **-en**.
- **Mixed** (~8): *bringen/brachte/gebracht*, *denken/dachte/gedacht*.

**In the static/movement pairs the static verb is always STRONG and the movement verb always WEAK. The verb form tells you the case.**

## The zu-family

- **um…zu / statt…zu / ohne…zu** behave identically. SOLID.
- **plain zu** when a verb or noun demands the infinitive; **um…zu** for genuine purpose.
- **zu + adjective ALWAYS → um…zu**: *zu müde, um heute zu kochen*.
- **würde + BARE infinitive** — no *zu*.
- **Da-compounds**: *darauf, darüber, damit, dafür, davon, daran*. Opened 2026-08-26, **STILL NOT DRILLED**.
