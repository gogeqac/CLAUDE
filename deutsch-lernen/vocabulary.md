All separable, all weak.

- **anmachen / ausmachen** — lights and devices, colloquial. *Mach das Licht aus.*

- **aufmachen / zumachen** — doors and windows. *Mach bitte das Fenster zu.*
- **öffnen / schließen** — neutral to formal; also shops, accounts. **abschließen** = to LOCK with a key.
- Do not confuse with **aussteigen** (to get out of a vehicle).

*zuzumachen* and *einzuschalten* both produced wrong on 2026-08-26 — the zu-placement is the weak point, not the meaning.

# GRAMMAR NOTES ATTACHED TO THESE WORDS

- **Separable verb + zu**: zu goes between prefix and stem, one word — *anzukommen, aufzumachen, zuzumachen, abzuholen, anzurufen, einzuschalten, wiederzusehen, mitzunehmen*. TOP RECURRING ERROR: four misses on 2026-08-26.
- **The zu-family — um…zu / statt…zu / ohne…zu** all behave identically: comma, no subject, *zu*-infinitive at the end, subject inherited from the main clause. SOLID — produced correctly many times.
- **um…zu vs. plain zu**: plain *zu* when a verb or noun demands the infinitive (*versprechen, versuchen, anfangen, sich entscheiden, keine Zeit*). *um…zu* for genuine purpose.
- **damit**: required instead of *um…zu* when the two clauses have different subjects. Verb last. Produced correctly unaided in ex. 33.
- **Da-compounds**: a preposition-governing verb with a *zu*-clause object absorbs the preposition — *auf* → **darauf**, plus *darüber, damit, dafür, davon, daran*. Opened 2026-08-26, NOT YET DRILLED.
- **Fronted subordinate clause** → finite verb second, subject after it. Produced correctly three times, unprompted.
- **zu Hause** (location) vs. **nach Hause** (movement) — wrote *nach zu Hause*, conflating the two.
- **Modals: umlaut = hypothesis.** Präteritum LOSES the umlaut: musste, konnte, durfte, mochte. Konjunktiv II KEEPS it: müsste, könnte, dürfte, möchte. *wollen/sollen* have no umlaut so both forms collapse.
  - Error 2026-08-26: used *hätte* for "would have to" — the correct form is **müsste**, one word. German has dedicated Konjunktiv II modal forms; English builds them analytically.
  - Correct 2026-08-26: *konnte* (not *könnte*) for a plain past fact in ex. 33 — a distinction historically over-applied in the other direction.
- **Konjunktiv II conditional** — *Wenn ich mehr Zeit hätte, würde ich Italienisch lernen.* Produced perfectly unaided, both halves. **würde + infinitive** is the default, but *haben/sein/*modals prefer their own forms (*hätte, wäre, müsste, könnte*) over *würde haben/sein*.
- **Ersatzinfinitiv — CONFIRMED SOLID 2026-08-26.** A modal in a compound past does NOT become a Partizip II: **hätte + main verb infinitive + modal infinitive**. *Du hättest mich anrufen sollen*, never *angerufen gesollt*. Both variants produced correctly and unaided:
  - standard case (ex. 37)
  - **sein-verb exception (ex. 38)**: *Er hätte zu Hause bleiben sollen* — *bleiben* takes sein, but the modal overrides it and forces **hätte**. This had slipped in earlier sessions; now confirmed.
- **Two-way prepositions**: *in die Küche*, *ins Kino* (movement, Akk.) — handled correctly both times.
- **zu + adjective** always continues with *um…zu*: *zu dunkel, um hier zu lesen*; *zu müde, um heute zu arbeiten*. Two clean productions.
