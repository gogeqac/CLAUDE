**sich verabschieden von — the format effect again.** Failed at ex. 68 and ex. 119 as an infinitive prompt (verb only, no government). **Correct with all three parts at ex. 139** in the frame *Ich verabschiede mich ___ (from my colleagues)* → *von meinen Kollegen*.

Why the second half keeps dropping in the infinitive format: **two different cases in one clause.** The reflexive pronoun is accusative (*mich, dich, sich, uns, euch, sich*), the *von*-phrase dative on top.

Examples given 2026-08-29:
- *Ich verabschiede mich von meinen Kollegen.*
- *Er hat sich von seiner Familie verabschiedet, bevor er nach München gezogen ist.*
- *Wir müssen uns jetzt vom Chef verabschieden.* (von + dem → **vom**)
- *Sie verabschiedete sich am Bahnhof von ihrer Mutter.*
- Without an object the *von*-phrase drops entirely: *Ich möchte mich verabschieden.*

**Word-order note raised by Gonçalo:** *Sie verabschiedete sich von ihrer Mutter am Bahnhof* is grammatical, but a *von*-phrase bound to the verb sits closer to the end than a free adverbial — *Sie verabschiedete sich am Bahnhof von ihrer Mutter* reads better.

**Angst vor has now failed twice with the identical error (*von*).** Separating the two:
- **von** + Dat. = from, of
- **vor** + Dat. = before, in front of, and in fear expressions

## ein-words

| | masc | neut | fem | plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nom** | ein | ein | eine | keine |
| **Akk** | ein**en** | ein | eine | keine |
| **Dat** | ein**em** | ein**em** | ein**er** | kein**en** |

Only the masculine changes between Nominativ and Akkusativ.

## Adjective endings after an ein-word

The adjective picks up whatever the article failed to carry. **-en is the default**; only five slots deviate: masc nom *-er*, neut nom/akk *-es*, fem nom/akk *-e*. Everything else **-en**.

*-er* on the ADJECTIVE only ever appears in masculine nominative.

## Comparative + case ending stacked — STILL NOT DRILLED

*eine größere Wohnung*, *einen größeren Balkon*. Comparative **-er** on the stem, then the case ending on top. The trap: comparative *-er* looks like a finished adjective ending. Error at ex. 49, never re-tested.

# PREPOSITIONS

## Always Dativ

**aus, außer, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu** — never take any other case.

**bei** — the employer/premises preposition: *bei der Firma*, *beim Arzt* (at the doctor's) vs. *zum Arzt* (going there).

## Obligatory contractions — ERROR ex. 53

*von + dem* → **vom** · *zu + dem* → **zum** · *zu + der* → **zur** · *an + dem* → **am** · *in + dem* → **im** · *bei + dem* → **beim** · *an + das* → **ans** · *in + das* → **ins**

- **zur** = *zu + der* → FEMININE only · **zum** = *zu + dem* → masculine and neuter

Ex. 29 *zur Arzt* → **zum Arzt**, REPAIRED ex. 48. Ex. 53 *in dem Wohnzimmer* → **im Wohnzimmer** — obligatory, not optional.

## aus vs. von

- **aus** = emerging from *inside* an enclosed space: *aus dem Haus, aus der Tasche*
- **von** = away from a point or surface: *vom Bahnhof, vom Tisch*

## Two-way prepositions — A STRENGTH

Dativ for location (*wo?*), Akkusativ for movement (*wohin?*). **The verb decides.** Ex. 36 carried both in one sentence, unaided.

**Figurative direction also takes Akkusativ**: *sich freuen **auf die** Ferien* (ex. 140).

## Dative plural adds -n to the NOUN

Whenever the plural doesn't already end in *-n* or *-s*: *mit den Freunden, mit den Kindern, aus den Häusern, zwischen den Fenstern*. Dropped at ex. 57 after a numeral; **restored at ex. 137 and 139**.

## Countries and cities

**nach** for countries without an article and all cities: *nach Italien*, *nach München*. Exceptions take *in + Akk.*: *in die Schweiz, in die Türkei, in die USA*.

**A city cannot attach directly to the verb** — ex. 102 follow-up: *Ich ziehe nach München*, never *Ich ziehe München*.

## Time prepositions

**um** — clock times · **am** — days/parts of day · **im** — months/seasons · **seit** — + Dativ · bare — *nächste Woche, jeden Tag*

# VERBS

## Strong / weak / mixed

- **Weak** = regular. *legen – legte – hat gelegt*.
- **Strong** = irregular, stem vowel shifts. *liegen – lag – hat gelegen*. Participle in **-en**.
- **Mixed** (~8): *bringen/brachte/gebracht*, *denken/dachte/gedacht*.

**In the static/movement pairs the static verb is always STRONG and the movement verb always WEAK.**

## Epenthetic -e- after stems in -d / -t — explained 2026-08-29

When the stem ends in *-d* or *-t*, an *-e-* is inserted before the ending so it stays pronounceable:
- *verabschied-* → *er verabschiedet*, *er verabschiedete*
- *arbeit-* → *er arbeitet*, *er arbeitete*
- *find-* → *er findet* · *red-* → *er redete*

## verabschiedet vs. verabschiedete — asked 2026-08-29

| | |
|---|---|
| present | sie verabschiedet sich |
| Präteritum | sie verabschiedet**e** sich |
| Perfekt | sie hat sich verabschiedet |

The *-te* is the past marker; the final *-e* is the third-person singular ending. No *ge-* in the participle because *ver-* is inseparable. Präteritum is normal in written narrative; speech prefers the Perfekt.

## anfangen + zu-infinitive — FOURTH failure

Governs a **zu-infinitive**, not a bare one. The zu-infinitive sits **OUTSIDE the verbal bracket**, trailing after the finite verb: *bevor es angefangen hat zu regnen*, not *bevor es regnen angefangen hat* (that shape is the Ersatzinfinitiv, MODALS only).

Simpler routes: *bevor es anfing zu regnen*, or a prepositional phrase.

## Preposition vs. conjunction — the same word twice

- **nach** + Dativ → *nach dem Essen* · **nachdem** = conjunction, verb last
- **vor** + Dativ → *vor dem Haus* · **bevor** = conjunction, verb last

Error ex. 19. Still worth a dedicated drill.

## zu-placement — solid

Separable → **zu inside** (*abzuholen*). Inseparable → **zu in front** (*zu verabschieden*). **11/11 on 2026-08-27.**

## The zu-family

- **um…zu / statt…zu / ohne…zu** behave identically. SOLID.
- **zu + adjective ALWAYS → um…zu**: *zu müde, um heute zu kochen*.
- **würde + BARE infinitive** — no *zu*.
- **Da-compounds** (*darauf, darüber, damit, dafür, davon, daran*) — opened 2026-08-26, **STILL NOT DRILLED**.

## Verb forms

- **Subject–verb agreement** — four errors 2026-08-27, zero in sessions 9–11.
- **English bleeding in under load** — *Her*, *We*, *Munich*, *I* for *Ich* (ex. 56). None in sessions 10–11, but the isolated-item format carries little load. **He also wrote *Munich* for *München* at ex. 102 follow-up.**
- **Modals: umlaut = hypothesis.** Präteritum loses it; Konjunktiv II keeps it.
- **Ersatzinfinitiv** — solid. MODALS only.
- **es gibt** — forces Akkusativ.
