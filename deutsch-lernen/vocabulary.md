### Reflexive pronoun drop — recurring pattern

The pronoun vanishes under sentence load on **sich beeilen, sich anpassen, sich entscheiden**. Not a knowledge gap: on 2026-08-27 *uns* was retained correctly in ex. 7 and then *sich* was dropped in ex. 9. It goes when the sentence gets longer. Drill with long sentences specifically.

# GRAMMAR NOTES ATTACHED TO THESE WORDS

## zu-placement — THE TEST THAT WORKS

Does the prefix split off in the present tense?
- *Ich hole **ab*** → yes, separable → **zu goes inside**, one word: *abzuholen*
- *Ich **ver**abschiede mich* → no, inseparable → **zu goes in front**, separate word: *zu verabschieden*
- no prefix at all → *zu* in front: *zu kochen, zu ziehen, zu erreichen*

Separable forms drilled: *anzukommen, aufzumachen, zuzumachen, abzuholen, anzurufen, einzuschalten, auszuschalten, wiederzusehen, mitzunehmen*.

**STATUS 2026-08-27: 8/8 correct in session 8**, including all four verbs missed on 2026-08-26 (*zuzumachen, einzuschalten, anzurufen, anzukommen*). This was the top recurring error; it is now the strongest part of the topic. Re-test in a later session to confirm it holds.

## The zu-family

- **um…zu / statt…zu / ohne…zu** all behave identically: comma, no subject, *zu*-infinitive at the end, subject inherited from the main clause. SOLID.
  - Error 2026-08-27: *ohne sich **verabschieden*** — the *zu* was omitted entirely. The construction still needs it.
- **um…zu vs. plain zu**: plain *zu* when a verb or noun demands the infinitive (*versprechen, versuchen, vergessen, anfangen, sich entscheiden, keine Zeit*). *um…zu* for genuine purpose — test by whether English "in order to" sounds natural.
- **zu + adjective ALWAYS continues with *um…zu***: *zu müde, um heute zu kochen*; *zu dunkel, um hier zu lesen*; *zu kalt, um draußen zu essen*.
  - REGRESSION 2026-08-27: wrote *zu müde, heute zu kochen* without *um*. This had been produced correctly twice on 2026-08-26. Re-drill.
- **damit**: required instead of *um…zu* when the two clauses have different subjects. Verb last.
- **Da-compounds**: a preposition-governing verb with a *zu*-clause object absorbs the preposition — *auf* → **darauf**, plus *darüber, damit, dafür, davon, daran*. Opened 2026-08-26, STILL NOT DRILLED.

## Case and preposition notes

- **Obligatory contractions**: *von + dem* → **vom**, plus *zum, zur, im, am, ans, ins, beim*. Error 2026-08-27: wrote *von dem Bahnhof*.
- **Movement to a city takes *nach***, never *in*: *nach München ziehen*. Error 2026-08-27, second session running.
- **zu Hause** (location) vs. **nach Hause** (movement). There is no *von Hause*.
- **fragen + Akk.** vs. **antworten + Dat.** vs. **bitten + Akk. + um + Akk.**
- **verlassen** is transitive → **haben**, and takes a direct object with no preposition.
- **Two-way prepositions**: *in die Küche*, *ins Kino* (movement, Akk.) — handled correctly.

## Word order

- **Pronoun objects move left, ahead of time expressions**: *Ich rufe **dich morgen** an.* But a full noun object stays put: *Ich rufe **morgen meinen Bruder** an.*
  - Error 2026-08-27: wrote *morgen mich anzurufen* → **mich morgen anzurufen**.
- **Zeit vor Ort** (time before place).
- **Fronted subordinate clause** → finite verb second, subject after it. SOLID — produced correctly unprompted in three consecutive sessions.

## Verb forms

- **Modals: umlaut = hypothesis.** Präteritum LOSES the umlaut: musste, konnte, durfte, mochte. Konjunktiv II KEEPS it: müsste, könnte, dürfte, möchte. *wollen/sollen* have no umlaut so both forms collapse.
- **müssen conjugation**: irregular in the singular (*ich muss, du musst, er muss*), regular in the plural (*wir müssen, ihr müsst, sie müssen*). Error 2026-08-27: wrote *wir muss*.
- **Conjugation slips under load** — a recurring category, not a knowledge gap: *sie gehst*, *wir muss*. Both happened while attention was on the surrounding structure.
- **Konjunktiv II conditional** — *Wenn ich mehr Zeit hätte, würde ich Italienisch lernen.* **würde + infinitive** is the default, but *haben/sein/*modals prefer their own forms (*hätte, wäre, müsste, könnte*).
- **Ersatzinfinitiv — CONFIRMED SOLID 2026-08-26.** A modal in a compound past does NOT become a Partizip II: **hätte + main verb infinitive + modal infinitive**. *Du hättest mich anrufen sollen*, never *angerufen gesollt*. Includes the sein-verb exception: *Er hätte zu Hause bleiben sollen*.
- **Impersonal verbs** take the dummy **es**: *es regnet, es schneit, es gibt*.
